Questions
What is Tvarz? +
Tvarz is a free AI cinema channel streaming 24/7 — just like TV, but built with artificial intelligence. Free members watch the live stream only. Premium members can also pick 2 films a day from the Archive.
Do I need an account? +
No account needed for the free live stream. Sign in with Google to access Premium.
Why only 2 films a day? +
Tvarz is built around live, shared viewing — not an unlimited on-demand library. Premium gives you 2 picks a day from the Archive on top of the live channel, so there's always something new to look forward to.
Are the films really made with AI? +
Yes. Every title on Tvarz is produced with AI video tools — Seedance, Veo, Kling, and others.
